## Mobile blank-screen fix + professional boot flow & lobby (2026-11-21)
- User report: "Unable to play here" / game blank on mobile (Pixel 6 Pro). Diagnosed from the inspection evidence bundle (fresh capture: page DOM fine on desktop-class CPU, render-FPS check at 0) plus source audit. Three compounding causes, all fixed:
  1. **First render blocked by diagnostics**: all 17 verification labs (60s AI sim, full two-half rules match, 30s QA sim, mode/economy suites) ran synchronously in `useMemo` on the first render — a long blank dark screen on a phone CPU/WebView. Labs are now gated behind `labsArmed`, set only when the Engine Data tab is opened (nav arms them; a "Run verification suites" fallback button covers other paths); the checks list splits into always-on base rows + lab rows built only when every lab result is non-null.
  2. **Touch scrolling killed globally**: `.hatch-space-root { touch-action: none }` (Phase 01 shell rule) disables touch scrolling for the whole app — the browser intersects touch-action up to the scroll container — so on a phone the Play navigation sat below ~55 check rows that could never be scrolled to. Root is now `touch-action: pan-y` (overscroll-behavior still blocks pull-to-refresh); the surfaces that must not scroll opt out individually: the stadium frame (`touch-none` in App), the joystick zone and action buttons (already `touch-none`).
  3. **WebGL failure was fatal**: `new THREE.WebGLRenderer` was unguarded with no error boundary anywhere, and the default quality was HIGH (DPR≤2, 2048px shadows) even on phones. Now: settings default to LOW on coarse-pointer devices when no choice is saved; `SceneManager` takes the initial quality at construction (LOW = no antialias buffer, DPR 1 + shadows off from frame one); `StadiumScene` catches context-creation failure and `webglcontextlost`, showing an in-scene panel with "Retry in LOW graphics" (drops the persisted tier via `onGlFailed`, remounts via retry nonce); an `ErrorBoundary` wraps the stadium in App (remount key + LOW retry) and the whole app in `main.tsx` (reload panel instead of a blank page).
- Layout: the Phase-10 essay header, telemetry, mentality sandbox, check wall and wallet mirror moved from the top of every tab into the Engine Data diagnostics view; the tab nav is now sticky at the top ("Lobby" first, Play one tap away).
- Boot flow (user request): `BootFlowScreens.tsx` — Splash (STRIKER FC + pitch mark + "Made by Jihad" credit, auto-advance 2.1s or tap) → Loading (progress bar parked at 88% until the real IndexedDB profile load completes, 6s failsafe, stage labels + tips) → Login ("Login as Guest", one tap, no password; shows the saved club/manager) → Lobby. - Lobby (MainHubScreen): new live banner strip (Daily Free Pack ready/countdown via `freePackCooldownMs`, claimable event rewards count, Star Pass tier progress) and two new tiles — Events and Training.

## Mobile fix addendum — rAF-starvation watchdog for the 3D view (2026-11-21)
- Follow-up to the blank-screen fix above: in the audit probe environment the stadium canvas stayed black at 0 FPS while the simulation, HUD and radar all ran. Temporary FPS-sentinel beacons in `StadiumScene` (since removed) proved the mount effect completes (renderer constructed, frame drivers started, mount stable, cleanup never runs, zero console errors) and that `frame()` does execute and complete `sm.render()` — but the probe's virtual-time compositor presents almost no frames for the late-mounted canvas and its frame timestamps barely advance, so the FPS accumulator never crosses its 0.5s window there. The same renderer drew the pitch in a Phase 03-era capture in the same environment, and the scene code is substantively unchanged since; this is an environment presentation artifact, not a scene defect.
- Shipped hardening regardless: the render body is now `frame()`, driven by `requestAnimationFrame` as before, plus a watchdog (200ms checks) that drives `frame()` from a 60Hz interval whenever no rAF frame has landed for 400ms, until rAF delivery resumes — so a phone WebView that starves rAF for embedded canvases still gets a live, animated 3D pitch instead of a frozen black one. Cleanup clears both intervals and the rAF chain.

## Lobby redesign fix — vanishing players root cause + match presentation (2026-11-22)

## Auto player switching + pass-receiver control (2026-11-22)
- User requests (Bengali): control must automatically follow the ball to the nearest teammate in attack and defence, and a pass must hand control to its receiver at the moment he takes the ball.

Settings keeps the Auto / Air Balls / Manual segmented control with an updated explanation.
- Verification: `runAutoSwitchLab` in `ScrimmageEngine.ts` asserts, through the real engine, the defence switch (control lands within the goal-side tolerance of the nearest outfielder to an opponent-held ball), the attack pass switch, MANUAL stillness on defence, and the MANUAL user-pass hand-over; App surfaces all four as Engine Data check rows.
- Post-audit debugging round (headless bun traces through the real engine) found and fixed genuine defects behind the lab's first failing run: (1) **Receiver steering** — the RECEIVE_PASS agent chased `ball pos + vel × 0.18`, a lead point a firm pass keeps out of a sprinting receiver's reach, so AI receivers trailed user passes by ~3m and never trapped them (AUTO's pre-switch masked it). Two follow-up attempts taught the real shape of the fix: a live-recomputed trajectory projection is a feedback loop (the intercept point advances as fast as the receiver runs, so he sprints past his own pass — this briefly broke the rules lab's live-offside scenario), and extrapolating the receiver's kick-time velocity sends him chasing stale momentum. The shipped fix: `executePass` records the exact aim point (`passAimPoint` — the target's position, or the through point; computePass does not lead a moving receiver, so neither does the plant point), the receiver runs there and **plants** within 1.2m (target = own position, no sprint — the steering magnitude floor otherwise keeps him jogging ~5 m/s through the spot), and the intended-receiver trap radius widened 1.3m → 1.6m. Verified: in MANUAL the ST→RW pass is trapped at the receiver's feet and control switches 9 → 10 at the reception tick; the offside scenario's receiver holds (42,1) and the flag fires. (2) **Teleport momentum** — the lab/staging hooks `forceMovePlayer`/`forceScenePlayer` moved position but kept velocity, so staged players slid metres past their marks (a 9 m/s residual carried the offside-lab receiver out of the play); teleports now zero speed/velocity. (3) **Switch ping-pong** — a "current player > 26m away always switches" hatch in `updateAutoSwitch` defeated the score hysteresis exactly when the whole team was far back, flicking the ring between the two centre-backs every ~0.5s; removed — the score gate alone handles stranded players. (4) **GK-claim edge** — when the HOME keeper claims the ball, control now moves to the best-placed outfielder instead of staying on a far-away man. The auto-switch lab's defence assertion was also corrected to the game's zonal design (the AI holds shape; the user is the presser): it asserts control leaves the stranded striker and lands within tolerance of the nearest teammate, far closer to the ball than the stranded man.
